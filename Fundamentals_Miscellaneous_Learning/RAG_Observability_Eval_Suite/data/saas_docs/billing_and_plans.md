# CloudFlow Billing and Subscription Plans

## Overview
CloudFlow is an enterprise integration and orchestration platform. We offer three primary subscription tiers: Starter, Professional, and Enterprise.

## Subscription Tiers

### 1. Starter Tier
- **Cost**: $49 per month (billed monthly) or $470 per year (20% discount).
- **Included Workflows**: Up to 10,000 workflow executions per month.
- **Data Retention**: 30 days of execution logs and audit trails.
- **Support**: Community forums and standard email support (48-hour SLA).
- **Concurrency**: 5 concurrent active workflow runs.

### 2. Professional Tier
- **Cost**: $249 per month (billed monthly) or $2,390 per year.
- **Included Workflows**: Up to 100,000 workflow executions per month.
- **Data Retention**: 90 days of execution logs and trace telemetry.
- **Support**: Priority email and chat support (8-hour SLA during business hours).
- **Concurrency**: 25 concurrent active workflow runs.
- **Features**: Custom webhooks, role-based access control (RBAC), and team workspaces.

### 3. Enterprise Tier
- **Cost**: Custom pricing starting at $1,200 per month.
- **Included Workflows**: Unlimited or custom volume tier with reserved throughput.
- **Data Retention**: 365 days of execution logs with automated S3/GCS cold archiving.
- **Support**: 24/7 dedicated phone and Slack support with a 1-hour critical response SLA.
- **Concurrency**: 100+ concurrent active workflow runs.
- **Features**: Single Sign-On (SAML 2.0 / Okta / Azure AD), VPC peering, dedicated tenancy, and HIPAA/SOC2 compliance report access.

## Overage Fees & Billing Rules
- **Workflow Overages**: Extra workflow executions on Starter are billed at $0.008 per execution. On Professional, overages are billed at $0.004 per execution. Enterprise overage rates are governed by Master Services Agreement (MSA).
- **Refund Policy**: CloudFlow offers a 14-day money-back guarantee for initial annual subscriptions. Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing cycle begins.
- **Prorated Upgrades**: Upgrades between tiers take effect immediately and are prorated for the remaining billing cycle.
