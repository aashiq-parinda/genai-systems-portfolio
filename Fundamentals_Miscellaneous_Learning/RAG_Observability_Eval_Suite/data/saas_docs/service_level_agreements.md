# CloudFlow Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Incident Management

## Uptime Guarantees
- **Starter Tier**: 99.0% Monthly Uptime Percentage (No SLA credit penalty).
- **Professional Tier**: 99.5% Monthly Uptime Percentage.
- **Enterprise Tier**: 99.95% Monthly Uptime Percentage, backed by financial credits.

## SLA Credit Matrix (Enterprise Customers)
| Monthly Uptime Percentage | SLA Service Credit |
|---|---|
| < 99.95% and >= 99.0% | 10% credit of monthly invoice |
| < 99.0% and >= 95.0% | 25% credit of monthly invoice |
| < 95.0% | 50% credit of monthly invoice |

## Support Severity Tiers & Response Times
- **Severity 1 (Critical Down)**: Production completely halted. Enterprise response SLA: < 1 hour. Professional response SLA: < 4 hours.
- **Severity 2 (High Degradation)**: Major functionality impaired. Enterprise SLA: < 2 hours. Professional SLA: < 8 hours.
- **Severity 3 (Normal / Question)**: General inquiry. Response within 24 hours across all paid plans.
